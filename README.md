# HealthConnect_Clinic_Week8
## Improving Patient Appointment Attendance & Healthcare Support Using Data and AI
## Project Overview

HealthConnect Clinic is a fictional healthcare provider seeking to reduce missed appointments, improve attendance, make better use of appointment slots, and improve patient support.

The Week 8 project brought together the completed Data Analytics work into a final **Analytics & Decision Support Package**.

## Business Question

> **How can HealthConnect Clinic use data and AI to reduce missed appointments and improve the patient support experience?**

## Key Analytics Findings

* Overall no-show rate: **48.46%**
  
* Attendance rate: **46.28%**
  
* Average booking lead time: **29.64 days**
  
* Reminder coverage: **72.68%**
  
* No-show rates increased with longer booking lead times.
  
* Previous no-show history was associated with higher observed no-show rates.
  
* Analyzing lead time and previous no-show history together revealed higher-risk segments.
  
* Reminder patterns were examined as an operational factor.
  
* The **65+** and **Specialist Consultation** segments were further examined to support Data Science model interpretation.

## Final Dashboard
Power BI dashboard was refined in week 7 was rechecked to confirm validations.
The Dashboard findings were validated against the Python analysis.

## 🤝 Analytics & Data Science Integration

The final integration connected the **Data Analytics** findings with the **Data Science** modelling work.

Analytics findings on lead time, previous no-show history, and reminders were provided with regards to 65+ age segment and Specialist Consultation appointments provided by data science.

The collaboration followed:

**Test → Finding → Action → Retest → Validated Improvement**

## 💡 Final Recommendations

HealthConnect can consider:

* Monitoring appointments with long booking lead times.
  
* Using previous attendance history to support targeted appointment management.
  
* Reviewing reminder coverage and processes.
  
* Combining multiple risk indicators when prioritizing appointment support.
  
* Monitoring segments identified as requiring additional attention.

These recommendations are based on observed patterns and do not establish causation.
